# Brand

- Name **Stayshay** (wordmark lowercase "stayshay", Plus Jakarta Sans 800, tracking −0.04em).
- Positioning: "Trust made affordable" → hero hook **"Live like it's luxury. Pay like it's shared."**
- Palette: Forest Green `#145C46` (primary), Fresh Green `#27AE78` (accent/savings), Soft Mint `#DDF4E8` (verified, chips), Warm Ivory `#F4F2E9` (sections, sparingly), White cards, Deep Charcoal `#202B27` (text). Glow mint `#6EE7B7`.
- Font: **Plus Jakarta Sans** only (web `next/font`, app `@expo-google-fonts/plus-jakarta-sans`).
- Corners soft (12–32px), pill chips, real photos, generous whitespace.

## Shay (logo + AI character)
A soft green **squircle blob** (path `M58 4C92 2 116 20 116 56C116 94 94 116 60 116C25 116 4 96 4 60C4 26 24 6 58 4Z`), gradient `#3FD99A → #27AE78 → #145C46`, top-left shine, mint halo glow, two **glowing capsule eyes** (`#F4FFF9`).
Moods: idle (blink every ~4.8s, glance, outline morph, glow pulse), talking (eyes bounce), thinking (eyes look up/right, faster morph), happy (eyes become glowing arches ^ ^).
Files: web `apps/web/components/shay.tsx` + `.shay2*` CSS in `globals.css`; static `apps/web/public/brand/stayshay-mark.svg`; app `apps/mobile/components/shay.tsx`; icons generated by `apps/mobile/scripts/make-icons.mjs`.
**Rejected by the founder** (don't bring back): Google-maps-style pin with a face, realistic eyes, tails, plain circles, the generic Dubai skyline hero, "old Airbnb square boxes", grey map.

## Copy rules
Short, warm, concrete; prices in AED with thousands separators; "Never pay before viewing"; never claim government approval; badges describe Stayshay's checks only.
